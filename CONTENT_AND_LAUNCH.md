# Maathavam Innovation Labs — Website Content & Launch Notes

## Public positioning
- Brand line: INNOVATE TO IMPACT LIVES
- Supporting line: We build technology for hard problems.
- Position the company as an independent deep-tech innovation and IP origination company, not a generic IT services agency.
- Describe technology areas as territories of exploration and development. Do not imply every concept is a finished or validated product.

## Publishing rules
Before adding a case study, announcement, partner reference, patent statement, testimonial, logo or performance claim, obtain written company approval and verify factual accuracy, IP/disclosure status and image/brand permissions.
- Never publish confidential concept names, mechanisms, schematics, source code, sensor configurations, formulas, internal project status or unfiled invention details.
- Distinguish a provisional application, a filed application, an allowed application and a granted patent accurately.
- Do not invent client relationships, institutional endorsements, awards, certifications, dates, team credentials, revenue or performance metrics.
- Do not add empty announcement panels or placeholder cards to the public site. Add a section only when it has useful, approved content.

## Contact form status
- The contact form posts to FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint for delivery to founders@maathavaminnovationlabs.com and provides an on-page success/failure status.
- FormSubmit requires the recipient to verify/activate the address on its first use. The site owner must complete that activation from the founders inbox and run a real end-to-end test before launch.
- FormSubmit is a third-party processor; the privacy notice identifies this. Do not claim that delivery is live until recipient activation and a test email are confirmed.
- The client-side code contains no email service secret. The form includes browser validation and a honeypot field; provider-side anti-spam protections remain enabled.

## Launch checks
- Confirm legal entity name and approved company contact details.
- Review privacy notice against actual hosting, email and retention practices.
- Test navigation, all CTA links, form validation, keyboard focus, mobile navigation and reduced-motion behaviour.
- Check image loading, browser console, accessibility contrast, metadata, canonical URL, sitemap and robots directives.
- Run performance and accessibility checks on the deployed preview.
- Confirm hosting/account ownership and a rollback procedure.
- Obtain written approval of the preview before production deployment.
