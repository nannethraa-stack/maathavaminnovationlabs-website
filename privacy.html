Privacy & responsible contact

Your information.
Handled with care.

This page explains the information that may be involved when you visit the Maathavam Innovation Labs website or contact us through it. The site is intended for professional enquiries about technology, research, licensing and strategic collaboration.

Information you choose to provide

If you complete the enquiry form, the information entered is used to prepare an email to Maathavam Innovation Labs. The current form opens the email application configured on your device; it does not submit the form to a website database. Your email provider and the recipient's mail system will process the message under their own terms and practices.

Please do not include confidential invention details, unpublished patent material, personal health information, trade secrets or other sensitive technical information in an initial enquiry. We can discuss an appropriate process for exchanging sensitive material if needed.

Website and hosting data

When a website is delivered over the internet, the hosting, delivery and security providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time and requested pages in operational logs. The exact collection, retention and location of those logs depend on the hosting configuration and provider policies.

Cookies and analytics

The website source does not intentionally add advertising trackers or third-party analytics by default. Your browser, hosting provider or security services may still process technical data required to deliver and protect the site. This notice should be updated if additional analytics, embeds or tracking technologies are introduced.

How enquiries are used

Information in an enquiry is used to understand the requested conversation, respond where appropriate and consider a potential professional relationship. Do not send information that is unnecessary for that purpose.

Sharing and retention

Messages may be handled by the email and hosting services required to operate the communication. Retention depends on the systems used to receive and manage correspondence. This site does not currently provide a public account or database where visitors can manage submitted enquiries.

Your choices and questions

For questions about a website enquiry or this notice, contact partnerships@maathavam.com. Please do not use email to send sensitive invention details before an appropriate sharing process has been agreed.

Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website's data practices or service providers change. Before production launch, Maathavam should confirm the applicable legal entity, contact details, retention practices, hosting configuration and any legal review required for its operations.
